The App does not collect, process, transmit, or store personal information, analytics data, usage statistics, device identifiers, or tracking information.

Dropbox Integration

The App allows users to sync data with their own Dropbox account. This synchronization is initiated and controlled by the user.

Files synchronized through Dropbox are controlled by the user and are subject to Dropbox’s own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Dropbox Privacy Policy: https://www.dropbox.com/privacy

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on the user’s device unless the user chooses to synchronize it with their own Dropbox account. The developer of the App has no access to this data.

Aside from optional Dropbox synchronization initiated by the user, the App does not use third-party services that collect personal data.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Security

Because the App does not collect or store user data on external servers, there is minimal risk of server-side data exposure. Users are responsible for securing access to their own devices and Dropbox accounts.
